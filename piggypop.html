PiggyPop 💰

PiggyPop is a high-fidelity mobile budgeting app designed to help students and young adults manage their spending and savings through a simple, fun, and visually engaging experience.

Problem

Many students and young adults struggle to keep track of their daily expenses. Small purchases such as food, transport, and subscriptions often go unnoticed, causing users to overspend without realising it. Existing budgeting apps can feel too complex and overwhelming, discouraging consistent use.

Solution

PiggyPop simplifies budgeting by allowing users to easily track expenses, monitor spending habits, and set savings goals through an intuitive and user-friendly interface. The app encourages better financial awareness and helps users build healthier money management habits over time.

Design Focus

Designed as part of the C218 UI/UX Design for Apps module, the app features a fully customised UI kit, clean and playful visual styling, intuitive navigation, and engaging micro-interactions to create a smooth and enjoyable budgeting experience.

Key Screens

Dashboard, Expense Tracker, Budget Overview, Savings Goals, Transaction History

Tech Stack:

  • Design & Prototyping: Figma
  • UI Components & Assets: Unsplash images, Icons8
